# WeBot Offer Taxonomy Contract

Status: Phase 01 source-of-truth contract.
Scope: public service families, service assignment, public-safe copy patterns, and implementation-private copy guardrails.

## Family Order

All public catalog, dashboard, API catalog, and service-routing surfaces must use this family order:

1. Create & Polish
2. Gather & Extract
3. Media & Convert
4. Build & Automate
5. Growth & Market
6. Review & Decide
7. Career & Prep
8. Plan & Itinerary

## Service Families

### 1. Create & Polish

Purpose: turn rough ideas, source notes, and brand needs into polished reviewed outputs.

Assigned services:
- Greeting cards and image cards
- Thank-you cards and social posts
- Tone rewrites and copy polish
- Logo concepts and business card drafts
- Photo enhancement
- Presentations and graphs

Public-safe copy patterns:
- "We Bot You through polished deliverables that are reviewed before they leave."
- "Turn rough notes into presentation-ready copy, cards, graphics, and brand drafts."
- "Reviewed delivery keeps the output useful, clean, and ready to hand off."

### 2. Gather & Extract

Purpose: collect, clean, structure, and summarize information from files, sites, and notes.

Assigned services:
- Website research and source pulls
- Scraping and structured data extraction
- OCR, PDF extraction, and document cleanup
- Invoice and spreadsheet cleanup
- Source notes and structured exports
- Transcription

Public-safe copy patterns:
- "We gather the source material, extract what matters, and hand back a reviewed package."
- "Good for messy PDFs, research pulls, spreadsheets, and structured summaries."
- "You get source-aware outputs without needing to run the workflow yourself."

### 3. Media & Convert

Purpose: convert, transcribe, and prepare media assets for practical use.

Assigned services:
- Text-to-MP3 audio
- Video transcription
- Audio cleanup and format conversion
- File format conversion for customer-ready delivery
- Captions, summaries, and media notes

Public-safe copy patterns:
- "Convert text, audio, and video into files people can use right away."
- "Reviewed media jobs come back with clean files, notes, and a clear handoff."
- "Useful when the goal is a finished asset, not another tool to manage."

### 4. Build & Automate

Purpose: ship practical technical help, automations, workflows, and implementation handoffs.

Assigned services:
- Web development
- Code review and code writing
- Spreadsheet formulas and workflow automation
- Agent skills and runbooks
- Technical handoffs and implementation notes

Public-safe copy patterns:
- "We ship practical automation, code help, and technical handoffs."
- "Good for small web work, workflow cleanup, formulas, and agent-ready runbooks."
- "Reviewed delivery means the build path is checked before it reaches you."

### 5. Growth & Market

Purpose: create market-facing plans, content, and optimization work that helps a customer reach people.

Assigned services:
- SEO
- Marketing strategies
- Offer page briefs
- Campaign drafts and content calendars
- Audience and channel notes

Public-safe copy patterns:
- "Purpose-built agents help you move faster without buying a high-priced general model."
- "Turn market notes into SEO plans, campaign drafts, and sharper offer language."
- "Reviewed delivery keeps the strategy direct, usable, and grounded."

### 6. Review & Decide

Purpose: compare options, pressure-test claims, and produce decision-ready summaries.

Assigned services:
- Research and comparison briefs
- Decision memos and vendor summaries
- Executive briefs and fact-check packs
- Business planning
- Risk, tradeoff, and next-step notes

Public-safe copy patterns:
- "We turn open questions into reviewed decision support."
- "Good for vendor choices, business planning, tradeoffs, and fact-checking."
- "You get a clear recommendation path with the supporting notes attached."

### 7. Career & Prep

Purpose: help people prepare for work, applications, interviews, and professional presentation.

Assigned services:
- Resume review
- Interview preparation
- Cover letter and profile polish
- Role research and application notes
- Practice questions and prep briefs

Public-safe copy patterns:
- "Bring the role, your background, and the deadline; We Bot You through the prep."
- "Reviewed career work keeps the final materials practical and human-readable."
- "Useful for resumes, interview prep, and sharper application packages."

### 8. Plan & Itinerary

Purpose: create practical plans for travel, events, outings, and logistics.

Assigned services:
- Vacation itineraries
- Date nights
- Business get-togethers
- Road trips
- Reservation notes, backup ideas, and helpful supply notes

Public-safe copy patterns:
- "Tell us the trip, event, or outing; we turn it into a reviewed plan."
- "Good for vacations, road trips, date nights, and business get-togethers."
- "The handoff includes timing, options, and backup ideas when useful."

## Distinct Offers Outside The Family Lineup

Office Skill Packs are not service families. They stay as installable skill-pack products for Microsoft Office and Google Workspace users.

Custom Builds are not service families. They stay as scoped consulting/build work for connectors, custom workflows, special storage needs, production integrations, and larger implementation paths.

WeBot Studio keeps monthly access plus one-time reviewed jobs until a later approved migration changes that model.

## Public-Safe Copy Rules

Allowed public copy themes:
- "We Bot You."
- trained workflows
- job-ready agents
- reviewed delivery
- lower-cost purpose-built agents versus high-priced general models
- direct, confident, no-hype delivery language
- clear customer outcomes, expected handoffs, and review checkpoints

Public copy must focus on what the customer gets, what family the work belongs to, how reviewed delivery works, and where the customer starts.

## Implementation-Private Copy Guardrails

The following are implementation-private and must not appear in public catalog, checkout, dashboard marketing, SEO, or buyer-facing copy:
- local models
- local inventory
- frontier supervision
- internal prompts
- internal tools
- queue internals
- model/router/runtime implementation details
- private reviewer procedures
- private vendor, infrastructure, or orchestration details

Internal planning documents may discuss these topics when needed, but public-safe copy must describe outcomes and reviewed delivery without exposing implementation details.
