Straight answers before we talk.

How much does custom automation cost?

Custom work is priced by quote after we understand the workflow, deliverables, timeline, and support required. The first 30-minute consultation is free.

What can you automate?

Repeatable digital work is often a good starting point: documents, research, lead handling, files, images, and connected back-office steps. Feasibility depends on your tools, access, exceptions, and review requirements. We will tell you when a workflow needs technical review or is not a fit.

What happens to my files?

We use the information and files you provide to scope and deliver the work. Handling depends on the agreed workflow and providers. Do not send confidential or sensitive material through the website chat; we can discuss an appropriate intake path during scoping. See the privacy policy for current website handling.

Can I review it before launch?

Yes. We can review the automation with you in WeBot Studio. Studio is the review and testing environment here, not a separate product you need to buy.

Can you host or maintain it?

Optional hosting and support can be included in the scope and quote. Handoff and deployment options are agreed before launch.

Are skill packs the same as custom automation?

No. Skill packs are self-serve products you install and operate. Custom automation is scoped, built, reviewed, and optionally supported by WeBot.

What happens on the first call?

We discuss the current steps, tools, volume, desired result, timing, and constraints. You can also request a project quote when you book. We will recommend a next step rather than give an instant estimate.

Not sure if your workflow fits?

Ask WeBot is an automated first-line assistant for capability and fit questions. It does not provide pricing. For a detailed answer, book a call or email us.
