How we handle your data.

WeBot Agency operates webot.agency and publishes applications including CrazyCade (com.thiscrazylife.arcade). This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Effective: 2026-04-21
Last updated: 2026-06-27

Summary

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • CrazyCade shows no third-party ads, runs no analytics SDKs, and does not transmit your gameplay data off-device.
  • For webot.agency and webot.studio, we collect the contact, payment, intake, and job files you choose to provide so we can deliver the work you requested.
  • Stripe and Google Play handle payment details for their respective purchases; we do not receive full card numbers.

What CrazyCade collects

When you use CrazyCade:

  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive a purchase token and acknowledgement from Google so we can grant you access to the content you bought. We do not receive or store your payment method, full name, or billing address. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Play Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Unlock state is stored locally on your device (Android shared preferences, arcade_unlocks.xml). This file records which games you have unlocked. It is not transmitted off your device.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are embedded. We do not profile your play behavior.
  • No account or sign-in is required. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.
  • Crash diagnostics: Google Play may collect basic crash reports through the standard Android mechanism to help us fix stability issues. This is handled by Google; we see only aggregated, non-identifying reports.

Ask WeBot and consultation requests

Ask WeBot is an automated first-line assistant. Messages you type are sent to WeBot's server-side assistant so it can answer. The chat endpoint does not write a transcript database. Standard infrastructure logs may still record technical request metadata. Do not include confidential, sensitive, or payment information in chat.

If you explicitly choose a booking or quote handoff, the approved use-case summary is stored in this browser tab's session storage until the consultation page reads and clears it. Booking sends your name, email, selected time, automation summary, and quote-request intent to the booking service so Google Calendar and Gmail can create the invitation and confirmation.

We retain consultation and active-engagement information only as needed for the relationship, support, and applicable legal, tax, or dispute records. Contact us to request access or deletion.

When you use WeBot Studio or buy agency services

  • Account and contact details you provide, such as name, email address, company name, and project notes.
  • Job intake details, prompts, source material, uploaded files, links, examples, and deliverable preferences you submit for the work.
  • Payment status, selected product or one-time job, transaction identifiers, billing email, and receipt metadata from Stripe. Stripe processes card details and other sensitive payment information under its own privacy policy.
  • Active-job files are kept only while the job and review window need them, then deleted or expired unless we must keep limited records for legal, tax, dispute, support, or active-engagement reasons.

When you browse webot.agency

Standard hosting and security logs may record IP address, browser type, referring URL, and request metadata for abuse prevention and aggregate traffic analysis. The site uses Google Analytics; its current measurement behavior is subject to Google's own privacy terms. We do not sell browsing data or use it to build advertising profiles.

How we use data

  • Deliver services you requested (respond to consultations, run Studio jobs, provide agency deliverables, grant purchases in CrazyCade).
  • Process purchases, one-time jobs, subscriptions, receipts, refunds, support requests, and required business records.
  • Maintain and improve our products (fix crashes, respond to user reports).
  • Comply with legal obligations (tax records, fraud prevention).

Third parties

The small number of third parties that process data on our behalf:

  • Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases in CrazyCade.
  • Stripe — processes webot.agency and webot.studio payments, subscriptions, one-time jobs, receipts, and related checkout metadata.
  • Google Play — distributes the app and provides basic crash reporting.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts webot.agency; standard server logs apply.
  • Booking service providers — process booking details and create Google Calendar and Gmail invitations and confirmations when you choose a booking.
  • Our hosting / job intake provider(s) — stores Studio intake details, active-job files, and delivery records needed to complete the requested work.

These parties operate under their own privacy policies and our contractual terms.

Children

CrazyCade is appropriate for a general audience and does not knowingly target children under 13. We do not request age, identity, or account information. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data through the consultation form, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Delete unlock state: uninstall CrazyCade to remove all locally stored unlock data from your device.
  • Restore purchases: Google Play keeps a record of your entitlements. Reinstalling CrazyCade and tapping "Restore" will re-grant any games you previously bought under the same Google account.
  • Access or delete consultation and Studio data: email us at the address below; we will respond within 30 days.
  • Regional rights (GDPR, CCPA, etc.): if you are located in a region with applicable privacy law, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or port the personal data we hold about you. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, access controls, periodic review) to protect information we hold. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users in the event of a material breach as required by applicable law.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and — for significant changes — post a notice on webot.agency. Continued use of our services after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
